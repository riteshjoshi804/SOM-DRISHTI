## What the numbers mean

* Reprojection RMSE, median and cross-validated RMSE are measured on the tie points
  themselves; they indicate internal consistency, not ground-truth accuracy.
* Low coverage is the honest limitation of the LOW_CONFIDENCE cases: the tie points are
  real (tens of inliers vs. ≤ 0.004 expected by chance) but clustered, so the transform
  is extrapolated over most of the strip.
* The two FAILED cases are the most informative results for the next phase: classical
  SIFT finds no correspondence at all between these illumination/modality pairs.
